What's nearby

Within a 300-metre radius: Alchemy Saunas next door, plus CrossFit, boxing and Muay Thai, weightlifting, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, pilates, a pole dance studio, a tennis club, padel (opening soon), HBF Park Sports Stadium and high-density apartment housing.

Area outlook

The City of Vincent's North Claisebrook Planning Framework (2023) earmarks this precinct for medium- to high-density mixed use, including apartment buildings, and the two nearby concrete batching plants are approved to operate only until 31 December 2027. That means more residents on the doorstep, which suits meal prep, catering or a commercial kitchen.
